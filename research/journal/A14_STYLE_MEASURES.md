### 4.2 Where the topic-close set differs from the journal-wide profile (JOURNAL_PROFILE_SUSCOM paywalled pass: figures median 9, tables 4.5, captions 1 to 18 words, references 36 to 42, algorithm boxes 6 of 14, related work 13 of 14)
- Newer and longer: 7 of 10 are 2025 or 2026; T median 16 pages. The profile's 10 to 14 pages understates the topic-close set.
- Tables: T median 7.5 against the profile's 4.5 (T range 3 to 11).
- Captions: figure captions in T are longer (mean 21.6, up to 91 words) than the profile's 1 to 18; table captions median 34 words.
- Uncertainty: the profile found 0 of 20 papers showing any interval; in T, 5 of 10 show some spread (101216 t-CI in a figure; 101398 ± std and Bonferroni p; 101407 CI from three archetypes; 101470 ± std over five seeds; 101429 Monte Carlo n = 30). What is still absent: bootstrap, preregistration (0 of 10), requested-versus-actual runtime handling (0 of 10), and an oracle-versus-forecast decomposition.
- Limitations subsections are a 2026 pattern (4 of 6 articles from 2026, 0 of the 9 articles before 2026).
- The lane-A most-cited set (L) is algorithm and review work from 2023 to 2024 (Name-colon titles, 100988 and 100992 with 46 to 47 equations, 100949 and 100985 metaheuristics, two reviews with 180 and 188 references); it has no limitations section, data on request, and no spread statistics. Citations there come from reviews and metaheuristic scheduling, not from rigour, so the T set is the right model for format and Paper A should exceed it on method.

### 4.3 Answers to the brief's open gaps
- G3 (topic-close caption style, baselines, statistics, limitations): settled for all ten. Captions: figure median mean 21.6 words, 2 of 10 papers state findings with numbers in captions (101498: 45 to 85 % improvement, 6 to 12 %; 101398); table captions median 34 words. Baselines: 101470 Pred-RR, Pred-GR, E2E-RL, Oracle and ablations; 101398 Static, Energy-Aware, CASPER; 101498 Local baseline plus spatial, temporal, spatio-temporal arms; 101407 local-immediate baseline plus one number from CarbonFlex; 101216 two unnamed baselines; 101429 reference, rule-based and MPC; 100950 and 100592 one simple baseline. Statistics: see 4.2. Limitations: 4 of 10 have a subsection. Settled by 101498, 101470, 101398, 101429, 101216, 101407.
- G5 (highlights): not settled; no PDF shows highlights (0 of 16). Needs the ScienceDirect article pages (web), which the PDFs cannot supply. Keep R4 and the guide wording.
- G6 (abstract word range): settled enough. Median 216, T 220, IQR about 192 to 251; the 250 limit is not enforced at typeset stage (4 of 15 above 250, up to 275). Papers: 101398 (275), 100592 (265), 101216 (256), 100985 (251), 101470 (250). Keep 200 to 230 as the working range and stay under 250.
- G8 (algorithm box; separate Reproducibility subsection): algorithm boxes 8 of 15 (5 of 10 T); a dedicated Reproducibility subsection only in 101106 (5.1), a one-line reproducibility sentence in 101429, 101333, 101498. Neither is a rule; an algorithm box is common, a Reproducibility subsection is rare and therefore a differentiator.
- Side finding for G2: 101470's "Ideal (Oracle)" row is a perfect-information schedule in the scheduling comparison (Table 8), with six workload and wind-power datasets; none of the ten handles requested versus actual runtimes (0 matches for walltime or requested runtime). W7's "no requested-versus-actual runtimes" claim now rests on full texts of 101498, 101216, 101470, 101407, 100950 (and the other five).

### 4.5 What a best-in-journal Paper A looks like on these measures
- Title 13 to 16 words, descriptive, optional colon subtitle, no method acronym needed (101498 pattern: verb-led, no colon).
- Abstract 200 to 230 words, one paragraph, three to five numbers each with an interval (best peers carry 5 to 6 bare percentages and none an interval), one weak or null result, one operator implication.
- 5 to 6 keywords. Sections 1 Intro; 2 Related work with a feature comparison table (5 of 10 T have one: 101333, 101398, 101407, 101429, 101470); 3 Data; 4 Method; 5 Results; 6 Discussion with Limitations and threats to validity (4 of 6 articles in 2026) and a short Reproducibility subsection (1 of 15); 7 Conclusion.
- 3 nominal-voice contributions; "we" about 3 to 7 per 1,000 words; 9,000 to 11,000 words of prose, about 14 to 19 pages.
- 9 to 12 figures, 6 to 9 tables, 12 to 25 equations, one or two algorithm boxes, 40 to 55 references.
- Main results table in the 101470 layout (row groups Proposed / Oracle / Baselines / Ablation, mean ± spread, units in headers, note below) plus the 101398 layout (best bold, a test table) but with bootstrap intervals and Holm adjustment from the preregistration.
- Figures meeting all three rules, which no paper of the ten does: Okabe-Ito, 7 pt, no overlaps; one colour per arm across figures; interval on every estimate (101216 Fig. 6 and 101470 Fig. 7 as models); finding-stating captions of 20 to 35 words (101498 as precedent).
- Public Zenodo and GitHub links with checksums (only 101106 and 101498 do), declarations (CRediT, competing interest, AI, funding) in the order used by the 2026 papers: after the Conclusion, CRediT, Declaration of competing interest, Acknowledgements, Data availability, References.
